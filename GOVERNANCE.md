# Governance

## Maintainers

NVIDIA Health Validation is maintained by the dsx-ai-factory/health-validation-sw-maintainers team at NVIDIA. See
[`MAINTAINERS.md`](MAINTAINERS.md) for the current list of maintainers and
their GitHub handles.

## Decision Making

Day-to-day decisions (bug fixes, minor improvements, dependency updates) are
made by the maintainers via pull request review. A single maintainer approval
is sufficient for routine changes.

Significant changes — new check domains, breaking API changes, changes to
threshold governance policy, or changes to the verdict/exit-code contract —
require:

1. A GitHub Issue or Discussion opened for community input (minimum 5 business
   days open before merging).
2. Agreement from at least two maintainers.

## RFC Process

For major architectural changes, open an issue with the `rfc` label and prefix
the title with `[RFC]`. Describe the problem, proposed solution, and
alternatives considered. RFCs stay open for at least 10 business days.

## Adding Maintainers

New maintainers are nominated by existing maintainers and approved by a simple
majority. A nominee should have a track record of quality contributions and
demonstrated understanding of the project's design goals.

## Removing Maintainers

Maintainers who have been inactive for 6+ months may be moved to Emeritus
status after a private discussion. Emeritus maintainers are listed separately
in `MAINTAINERS.md`.

## Code of Conduct

All participants are expected to follow the [Code of
Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Enforcement is handled by the maintainer team.
